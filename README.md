# Court Kings

Retro pixel basketball franchise game. Coach a team, play the big possessions on offense and defense, sim the rest, and build a dynasty: training, facilities, trades, draft, free agency, owner and fans.

Play: https://treyblade9to10-eng.github.io/court-kings/

Controls: drag left side to move, hold SHOOT and release at the top of the meter, tap a teammate or PASS, MOVE for a crossover. On defense: CONTEST and STEAL. Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space, K, L, P.

v2: landscape side-view court, real 2026-27 NBA rosters (or made-up players), team logos loaded from ESPN's CDN (can switch to retro letters in Office > Settings), works on phones (sideways) and computers.

Code, pixel art (drawn in code) and sounds (synthesized) are original. NBA team names and logos belong to the NBA and its teams; this is a free fan project, not affiliated with the NBA. Fonts: Pixelify Sans and Press Start 2P (SIL Open Font License).
